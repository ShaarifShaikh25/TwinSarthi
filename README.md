# ❄️ TwinSarthi  
### Digital Twin for Antarctic Research Station Management

---

## 🌍 Overview

TwinSarthi is a Digital Twin platform designed to enable **efficient remote monitoring and management of Antarctic research stations**.

It acts as a **virtual assistant ("Sarthi") for station operations**, providing real-time insights, predictive analytics, and intelligent decision support.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Antarctic research stations face:

- Extreme temperatures (-50°C)
- Limited communication bandwidth
- Remote and isolated operations
- Fragmented monitoring systems
- Lack of predictive maintenance and alerts

---

## 💡 Our Solution

TwinSarthi transforms physical stations into **intelligent digital replicas**, enabling:

- Real-time monitoring of station systems  
- AI-driven alerts and anomaly detection  
- Predictive insights (fuel, energy, failures)  
- Smart decision support for operators  

---

## 🧠 Key Features

- 📊 Live Monitoring Dashboard  
- 🌡️ Environmental Tracking (Temperature, Wind, Humidity)  
- ⚡ Energy Monitoring (Solar, Battery, Generator)  
- 📦 Inventory Management (Fuel, Food, Medical Supplies)  
- 🚨 Intelligent Alerts System  
- 🔮 Predictive Analytics  
- 🤖 Priority-Based Decision Engine  
- 👨‍💻 Human-in-the-Loop Control
